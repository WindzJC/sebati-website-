The storyteller

Sebati Edward
Mafate.

Novelist. Memoirist.
Independent filmmaker.

“I love telling a story, either on paper, the big screen, or around a fire.”

Born in Zambia and raised in Botswana and South Africa, Sebati writes across fiction and memoir, and works in independent film.

At Lotsane, a teacher encouraged his writing and brought him into the drama club. His school experiences later became Memories of Lotsane: The Chronicles of an African Boarding School.
