Dumisani was adopted at birth. He and Sasha grow up together in Botswana, the sons of American documentary filmmakers. When poachers kill their parents, the brothers leave for the United States.

They are looking for a new beginning.

ZAMBIA → BOTSWANA & SOUTH AFRICA

A life in writing.
A practice in film.

Born in Zambia and raised in Botswana and South Africa, Sebati Edward Mafate is a novelist, memoirist and independent filmmaker.

At Lotsane, his teacher encouraged his writing and brought him into the drama club. Years later, those school experiences became Memories of Lotsane: The Chronicles of an African Boarding School, published in 2012.
